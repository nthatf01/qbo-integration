Disconnecting the Integration

Sam Aguiar Injury Lawyers · internal integration

This integration is a private, internal tool for Sam Aguiar Injury Lawyers. To revoke its access to the Firm's QuickBooks Online company:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as a company administrator.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon) → Apps, or open My Apps.
  3. Find this integration in the connected apps list and choose Disconnect.

Once disconnected, the integration can no longer read from or write to the QuickBooks Online company until it is authorized again. Disconnecting does not delete any records already written to QuickBooks Online or Salesforce.
