Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the internal software integration (the "Integration") operated by Sam Aguiar Injury Lawyers (the "Firm") handles data. The Integration is a private back-office tool, not a consumer-facing service.

Data the Integration accesses

  • From the Firm's Salesforce/Litify org: disbursement and expense records, payee information, matter references, amounts, dates, and payment status.
  • From the Firm's QuickBooks Online company: vendor, customer, account, check, and bill records needed to record those disbursements.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to record the Firm's approved disbursements as checks and bills in the Firm's own QuickBooks Online company, and to write the resulting check number, date, and payment status back to the Firm's Salesforce records. The Integration does not use the data for any other purpose.

Data sharing

The Integration does not sell, rent, or share data with any third party. Data moves only between the Firm's own Salesforce and QuickBooks Online accounts, through those platforms' official APIs (operated by Salesforce and Intuit). No data is used for advertising.

Storage and retention

The Integration stores only operational state needed to run reliably (record identifiers and sync status) on systems controlled by the Firm. The authoritative records remain in Salesforce and QuickBooks Online and are retained under those systems' and the Firm's own retention practices.

Security

Access credentials are held only by the Firm and are not exposed to any outside party. Access to the Integration is limited to authorized Firm personnel.
