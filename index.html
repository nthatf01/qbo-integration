QuickBooks Online – Salesforce Integration

An internal back-office integration for Sam Aguiar Injury Lawyers.

This is a private, internal software integration operated by Sam Aguiar Injury Lawyers ("the Firm"). It automatically synchronizes the Firm's own financial and matter data between the Firm's Salesforce / Litify org and the Firm's QuickBooks Online company.

It is not a public application and is not offered as a service to anyone outside the Firm. There is no sign-up and no public user interface; access is limited to authorized Firm personnel.

What it does

  • Reads approved disbursement and expense records from Salesforce/Litify.
  • Creates the corresponding checks and bills in the Firm's QuickBooks Online company.
  • Writes the resulting check number, date, and payment status back to Salesforce.
